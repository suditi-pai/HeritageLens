Why preservation matters

Many folk art forms are passed on by families and villages, often by women. When younger generations lose touch, techniques and stories disappear. Recognizing and explaining these arts makes them easier to learn and value.

About

HeritageLens pairs computer vision with a curated knowledge base to help students, tourists and researchers identify and understand traditional Indian art. It was built to make cultural heritage easier to discover, and to show how AI can support preservation and education.

AI predictions can be wrong, especially for photos of unusual crops, mixed styles or modern reinterpretations. Treat results as educational guidance and confirm with an expert or a museum source.
